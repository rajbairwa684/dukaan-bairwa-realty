About The Project

Raghav Town Jaipur

Raghav Town is a premium gated residential plotted township by Kedia YBL Group, strategically located at Sirani, 100 Ft Road, Ajayrajpura, Kalwara, Jaipur, near Mahindra SEZ.


Spread across approximately 40 Bigha, the project is planned as a gated residential township offering residential plots along with planned roads, green spaces, security, utilities and lifestyle amenities.


The project is positioned in the Jaipur West–Kalwara–Mahindra SEZ corridor and is designed around the concept of comfortable, secure and community-oriented living.
