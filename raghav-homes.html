About The Project

Raghav Homes Jaipur

Raghav Homes Jaipur is a premium residential township located at Jaabad, Kalwara Road, Jaipur, near Mahindra SEZ and the 250 Ft Airport SEZ Road.


The project is situated on Main 82 Ft Road and offers residential plots with wide-road connectivity, landscaped green spaces, lifestyle amenities and a gated township environment.


The project is positioned in the developing Jaabad–Kalwara Road corridor, providing connectivity towards Mahindra SEZ, Kalwara, Ring Road, Bhankrota, Ajmer Road and surrounding industrial and commercial areas.
