🏡 Janardana City Jaipur – A New Address for Premium Living & Investment

Jaipur's western growth corridor is attracting increasing attention from homebuyers and property investors looking for well-connected residential destinations with modern infrastructure.

Janardana City is positioned as a premium gated township on Main 200 Feet Khatwara Road, before Jayshree Periwal International School, with connectivity towards Ring Road, Ajmer Road, Jaisinghpura, Bhankrota and Mahindra SEZ.

Designed around the concept of sustainable and comfortable community living, the project combines residential plots with landscaped surroundings, planned roads and lifestyle-focused facilities.
