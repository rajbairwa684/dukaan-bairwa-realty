🏡 About Janardana City

Janardana City is a premium gated township located on Main 200 Feet Khatwara Road, Jaipur, before Jayshree Periwal International School.

The project is positioned in Jaipur's developing western corridor with connectivity towards Mahindra SEZ, Ring Road, Ajmer Road, Jaisinghpura and Bhankrota.

The township concept focuses on residential plots, planned roads, green spaces, security and modern infrastructure.
