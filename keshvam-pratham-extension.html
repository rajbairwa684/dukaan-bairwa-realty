Keshvam Pratham Extension – Premium Gated Township Near Mahindra SEZ, Jaipur

Jaipur's western growth corridor is emerging as an important destination for people looking for residential plots, commercial opportunities and long-term property options.

Keshvam Pratham Extension is a proposed approximately 40 Bigha gated township located near Mahindra SEZ, Kalwara, with a major location advantage on the 250 Ft SEZ Road.

As per the project information supplied to BAIRWA REALTY, the project is positioned approximately 50 metres inside the 250 Ft Road near Neerja Modi School and approximately 1.5 km towards Bagru from the Mahindra SEZ T-Point.

The project concept focuses on planned roads, green areas, security infrastructure, underground utilities and both residential and commercial plot opportunities.
