BAIRWA REALTY & INFRA • Jaipur, Rajasthan

Keshvam Pratham Extension – Premium Gated Township Near Mahindra SEZ, Jaipur

Jaipur's western growth corridor is rapidly emerging as one of the city's most interesting destinations for residential and investment properties.

Keshvam Pratham Extension is a proposed 40 Bigha gated township located near Mahindra SEZ, Kalwara, with a major location advantage on the 250 Ft SEZ Road.

The project is positioned just around 50 metres inside the 250 Ft Road near Neerja Modi School and approximately 1.5 km towards Bagru from the Mahindra SEZ T-Point, as per the project information provided.

With planned roads, landscaped areas, security infrastructure and multiple residential and commercial plot options, Keshvam Pratham Extension is positioned for buyers looking for a well-planned township in the Mahindra SEZ growth corridor.

Project at a Glance:
Approx. 40 Bigha gated township • Near Mahindra SEZ • 250 Ft SEZ Road • Residential & Commercial Plot Options

📍 Prime Location Near Mahindra SEZ

Location is one of the strongest highlights of Keshvam Pratham Extension.

The project is situated at:

Project Location 250 Ft. SEZ Road, Near Mahindra SEZ, Kalwara, Jaipur

Its location provides access towards some of the major growth areas of western Jaipur.

Key Location Advantages

  • Approximately 50 metres inside the 250 Ft SEZ Road
  • Approximately 1.5 km from Mahindra SEZ T-Point towards Bagru
  • Near Mahindra SEZ
  • Close to Ajmer Road
  • Near Ring Road connectivity
  • Convenient access towards Kalwara and Bagru
  • Located in Jaipur's developing western corridor

The project brochure highlights its position as a location offering prime connectivity and planned living.

🏙️ 40 Bigha Gated Township

Keshvam Pratham Extension is spread across approximately 40 Bigha and is planned as a gated township with residential and commercial opportunities.

The township concept focuses on:

  • Planned internal roads
  • Gated entry
  • Security
  • Green spaces
  • Community facilities
  • Underground infrastructure
  • Residential plots
  • Commercial plot options

For buyers who prefer a planned township environment over an isolated plot, these features can be an important consideration.

💰 Plot Prices at Keshvam Pratham Extension

As per the project details supplied for this article, the indicative pricing is:

Plot Type Road Advertised Rate
Residential Plot 30 Ft Road ₹39,900/sq. yd.
Residential Plot 60 Ft Road ₹47,900/sq. yd.
Commercial Plot – Old Township 60 Ft Road ₹49,900/sq. yd.
Commercial Plot – New Township 60 Ft Road ₹49,900/sq. yd.
Important: Prices and availability can change. The final price may also vary depending on plot location, size, road-facing preference, PLC, development charges, taxes, registration and other applicable costs. Confirm the latest cost sheet before booking.

🌴 Premium Township Facilities

Keshvam Pratham Extension is designed around the idea of a secure, green and community-oriented residential environment.

The project information highlights a wide range of planned facilities.

🚧 Grand Entry Gate & Security

A grand entrance with a boom barrier provides controlled access to the township.

  • Grand Entry Gate
  • Boom Barrier
  • Security Guards
  • CCTV Surveillance
  • Block Boundary Fencing
  • 6-Foot Block Boundary Wall
  • 7-Foot Plastered Boundary Wall
  • Tower Lights in Common Areas

🌳 Landscaped Green Spaces

Green infrastructure is another major feature of the township.

  • Landscaped Gardens
  • Plantation
  • Roadside Plantation
  • Seating Gazebo
  • Meditation Zone
  • Common Green Areas

These spaces are designed to create a more pleasant environment for residents and families.

🛕 Community & Spiritual Spaces

The township also incorporates spaces intended for community and spiritual activities.

  • Temple
  • Jain Temple
  • Community Hall
  • Meditation Zone
  • Seating Gazebo

🏋️ Lifestyle & Family Amenities

Keshvam Pratham Extension includes recreational facilities intended for different age groups.

🛝 Kids' Play Area

Dedicated play swings provide recreational space for children.

🧘 Meditation Zone

A dedicated meditation area is planned for residents looking for a peaceful space.

🏋️ Open-Air Gym

An outdoor gym provides an option for residents to maintain an active lifestyle without leaving the township.

🏡 Community Hall

The community hall can serve as a common gathering space for residents and social functions.

💧 Underground Infrastructure & Modern Utilities

The project highlights underground utility infrastructure designed to provide a cleaner and more organized township environment.

Infrastructure Highlights

  • Underground Sewerage Line
  • Underground Water Pipeline
  • Underground Electrification
  • Overhead Water Tank
  • Rainwater Harvesting System
  • Interlock Tiled Roads
  • Fancy Electricity Poles
  • Street Lighting
  • Tower Lights in Common Areas

The combination of planned roads and underground services is intended to support the township's long-term infrastructure requirements.

🛣️ Wide & Planned Roads

Road connectivity is a major selling point of the project.

  • 30 Ft residential roads
  • 60 Ft residential/commercial road frontage
  • 250 Ft SEZ Road connectivity

Wide road networks can be beneficial for accessibility, traffic movement and future commercial activity.

🏢 Residential & Commercial Opportunities

One of the interesting aspects of Keshvam Pratham Extension is the availability of both residential and commercial plot options.

🏠 Residential Plots

Residential plots are positioned for buyers who want to:

  • Build their own home
  • Create a future residence
  • Purchase land for long-term holding
  • Live close to the Mahindra SEZ growth corridor

🏪 Commercial Plots

Commercial plots on the wider road network can be considered by buyers looking for:

  • Shops
  • Offices
  • Local businesses
  • Service establishments
  • Future commercial development

Commercial use, construction permissions and applicable regulations should always be verified before purchase.

📈 Why Consider Keshvam Pratham Extension for Investment?

There are several factors that make this project worth exploring.

1. Mahindra SEZ Proximity

Being near Mahindra SEZ gives the location exposure to an established employment and industrial ecosystem.

2. 250 Ft SEZ Road

The project's connection to the 250 Ft SEZ Road is one of its key location advantages.

3. Western Jaipur Growth Corridor

Kalwara, Ajmer Road, Bagru and the surrounding western Jaipur region have been developing as important residential, industrial and commercial zones.

4. Residential + Commercial Options

The availability of both residential and commercial plots gives buyers different options depending on their requirements.

5. Planned Township Infrastructure

Gated access, landscaping, internal roads, security and underground utilities can make a planned township attractive to end-users as well as long-term buyers.

Investment Note: Property appreciation is not guaranteed. Buyers should independently evaluate location, legal title, approvals, development progress, market demand and future infrastructure before investing.

  • Ajmer Road
